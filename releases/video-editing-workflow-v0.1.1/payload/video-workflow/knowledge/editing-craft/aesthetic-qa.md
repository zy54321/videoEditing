# Aesthetic QA — Common Craft

## Four-Level Rule Hierarchy

### LEVEL 1 — STRUCTURAL_INVARIANT

Must pass. Not negotiable by Style or taste.

Examples:

- Subtitles must not overflow screen (functional delivery)
- No unintended black frames / timeline gaps in deliverable
- Frozen content must not be altered
- Output must include required primary audio

Failure → **BLOCK** or mandatory repair.

---

### LEVEL 2 — REVIEWED_INVARIANT

Strong professional requirements; failure needs repair or explicit Owner acceptance.

Examples:

- Shots should serve a narrative function
- Attention target should be identifiable
- Composition should not destroy subject readability
- Color should not gratuitously destroy skin tone
- Audio hierarchy must be intelligible

---

### LEVEL 3 — CRAFT_DEFAULT

Professional best practice — **may be broken with explicit reason**.

Examples:

- Avoid consecutive functionally duplicate shots
- Normalize obvious unintended slow motion in observational footage
- Establish context before dense detail (when story needs it)
- Prefer motivated cuts over random timing

---

### LEVEL 4 — TASTE_OPTION

True style preference — not fake objectivity.

Examples:

- warm vs cool
- restrained vs energetic
- clean vs textured
- minimal vs expressive graphics
- stable vs handheld feel

**Do not** encode Taste Options as hard QA gates without Owner request.

---

## Application

| Layer | Uses hierarchy |
|-------|----------------|
| L3 Universal QA | Level 1–2 emphasis |
| L2 Style QA | Level 2–4 per genre |
| Owner Review | Level 4 and subjective motion / taste |

---

## Safety Above Aesthetics

Factual integrity and content authority override aesthetic preference.
