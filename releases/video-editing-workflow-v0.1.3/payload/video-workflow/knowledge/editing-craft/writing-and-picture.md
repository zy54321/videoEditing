# Writing & Picture — Common Craft

## Four Channels

| Channel | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Narration / copy** | Context, meaning, interpretation, invisible information |
| **Picture** | Evidence, atmosphere, action, contrast, concrete detail |
| **Original sound** | Authenticity, presence, environment, emotion |
| **Subtitles** | Communication + typography layer — not mechanical narration duplicate |

These channels **must not** be designed in isolation.

---

## Redundancy Rule

If the picture already expresses information clearly, narration should not mechanically repeat it.

Narration is for what **cannot** be shown:

- context
- interpretation
- invisible rules
- abstract comparison

Picture is for what **should** be shown:

- evidence
- place
- behavior
- contrast in action

---

## Subtitles vs Narration

Subtitles serve **readability and communication**, not verbatim duplication of every spoken word when pacing or layout requires segmentation.

Semantic authority (Frozen Script / Voice) governs **content**; presentation may adjust cue boundaries and line breaks without changing meaning.

---

## Original Sound

Original sound moments:

- reset rhythm
- increase authenticity
- let environment speak

May coexist with reduced or absent captions when intentional.

---

## Factuality

Picture must not imply false evidence.

Generic B-roll cannot stand in for specific real events without narrative framing.
