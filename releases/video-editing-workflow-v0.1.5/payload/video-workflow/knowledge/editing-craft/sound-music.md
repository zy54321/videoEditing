# Sound & Music — Common Craft

## Sound Layers

- **Narration / dialogue** — primary meaning carrier in many formats
- **Production sound / ambience** — place and authenticity
- **Room tone / silence** — rhythm and breath
- **SFX** — emphasis, transition, realism (use sparingly)
- **Music** — emotional arc and pacing support

---

## Hierarchy

Typical intelligibility order for narration-driven work:

```
Narration > essential dialogue > critical SFX > music bed > ambience
```

Dynamic range must remain listenable — no clipping, no unintended masking.

---

## Music Is Not Decoration

Music carries **narrative function**:

- entry
- sustain
- duck under voice or original sound
- turn / second theme
- ending fade

**Music arc** should align with beat structure, not loop blindly under entire timeline.

---

## J-cut / L-cut (Audio)

Audio may lead or trail picture to smooth rhythm.

Sound design and picture editing are **peer** narrative languages.

---

## Voice Treatment

- clarity and intelligibility first
- light corrective processing acceptable
- stylized FX only when narratively motivated
- frozen voice masters — non-destructive processing only

---

## Ducking

Lower music under narration or important diegetic sound.

Verify by ear — fixed dB recipes are guides, not laws.
