# Color / Look / Effects — Common Craft

## Technical vs Creative

| Phase | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Technical correction** | Exposure, white balance, shot match |
| **Consistency** | Coherent palette across sources |
| **Creative look** | Mood, polish, genre identity |

### Order

```
exposure / WB → shot match → consistency → creative look
```

---

## Color Craft

- exposure and highlight/shadow balance
- white balance believability
- skin tone plausibility (when people present)
- saturation discipline
- warm/cool relationships
- palette coherence across mixed sources

**LUT** is a tool — not automatic “better.”

---

## Effects Craft

Effects include:

- vignette
- grain / texture
- push-in / subtle zoom
- graphical emphasis
- Fusion / compositing treatments

### Motivation required

Effect must serve:

- narrative clarity
- emotional emphasis
- rhythm

**“More cinematic” or “more advanced” alone is not sufficient reason.**

---

## Restraint Default

When unsure, prefer invisible correction over obvious stylization.

Style Skill decides how much restraint applies per genre.
