# Observational Info Short — Directing

Common craft baseline: `video-workflow/knowledge/editing-craft/directing-story.md`

---

## Narrative Rhythm

Default pacing: **naturally brisk** — not urgent news, not slow cinematic prose.

### Structure

```
Hook → Establish → Observation 1..N → Reframe / Insight → Natural Close
```

### Hook (first 3–5s)

Establish at least one of: place, question, contrast, strongest information point.

Avoid: long logo, information-free slow opener.

### Beat Map (Blueprint)

Each beat should declare:

- `narrative_function` (CONTRAST, EVIDENCE, REVEAL, …)
- `visual_intent`
- `attention_target`
- `audience_effect`

Shot Plan optional unless beat is visually critical (see blueprint reference).

---

## Shot Function (this Style)

Prefer functions that serve **information + observation**:

| Function | When to use |
|----------|-------------|
| ESTABLISH | Place, context, “where we are” |
| EVIDENCE | Show the fact narrated |
| REVEAL / CONTRAST | Expectation vs reality (e.g. Sunday closure) |
| REACTION | Human response, social nuance |
| BREATH / ORIGINAL_SOUND | Authentic ambient moment |
| ENDING | Natural life image + brief tail |

Reject shots that only “look cinematic” without narrative job.

---

## Source Selection

Priority:

1. Real B-roll directly related to narration facts
2. Semantically relevant generic lifestyle B-roll
3. Atmosphere B-roll
4. AI supplement (last resort — never fake news / specific real events)

Filename is hint only — inspect content.

### Stock red flags

Lower priority: obvious slow motion, ad-like posing, over-cinematic look, realism mismatch.

---

## Author Intent (Style rules)

- Global intent may shift warmth, restraint, or emphasis within Style identity
- Local intent (per beat) may request e.g. “more contrast here” or “quieter captions in this section”
- Must not turn observational short into trailer, news, or vlog without Owner explicitly changing Style

---

## Repetition

Highly sensitive to stock repetition.

Reuse requires changed source range, framing, or narrative role — not timeline reposition alone.
