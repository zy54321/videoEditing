# Observational Info Short — Sound & Music

Common craft baseline: `video-workflow/knowledge/editing-craft/sound-music.md`

---

## Voice Treatment

Default: **Natural Voice** — clear, intelligible, narration-dominant.

Optional: light EQ, compression, warmth, de-ess, mild timbre treatment, radio/phone section when narratively needed.

**Frozen Voice Master:** non-destructive or derived audio only — never overwrite master.

---

## Sound Design

Restrained:

- environment / original sound preferred over decorative whoosh
- transition SFX sparingly
- emphasis SFX sparingly
- intentional silence OK

Not every cut needs SFX.

---

## Music Strategy

Music carries **narrative function**, not decoration.

**Default: ON** unless Owner disables → `OWNER_DISABLED` (not SKIPPED defect).

### Section roles (~60–90s)

| Section | Role |
|---------|------|
| Hook | light entry |
| Main | low-presence bed |
| Original-sound moment | duck / drop |
| Narrative turn | second track allowed |
| Ending | natural fade |

Multiple tracks, segments, crossfades allowed.

### Intake without music

Offer: A) Owner adds music, or B) disable for production.

**Must not** fetch unknown-copyright music or fake library automation without VERIFIED L1 route.

---

## Audio Mix

**Narration always wins.** Music below voice. Verify by listening.

Check: intelligibility, masking, clipping, unintended silence, abrupt music in/out.

Short fades preferred over hard music cuts.
