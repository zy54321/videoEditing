# Observational Info Short — Color & Effects

Common craft baseline: `video-workflow/knowledge/editing-craft/color-look-effects.md`

---

## Color / Look Strategy

Target: **Natural, Clean, Coherent, Slightly polished.**

- shot-to-shot consistency
- reasonable exposure / white balance
- believable skin tone when people present
- controlled saturation, mild contrast
- subtle warmth when appropriate

**Natural ≠ no processing.** Use only **VERIFIED** L1 color routes.

**Default prohibit:** heavy teal-orange, extreme LUT, crushed blacks, heavy grain — unless Brief requests.

---

## Effects Strategy

Default: **minimal.**

### Allowed

- subtle push-in / gentle zoom on static shots
- light vignette when justified
- small emphasis animation
- restrained blur for privacy / background
- simple motion graphics

### Rules

- never zoom every shot
- motion blur only when speed change genuinely requires it
- third-party OFX only if Runtime already has them — do not auto-install

Effect must serve narrative or rhythm — not “prove features exist.”

---

## Technical vs Creative

Follow craft order: correction → match → consistency → light creative look.

This Style stays closer to **correction + light polish** than heavy stylization.
