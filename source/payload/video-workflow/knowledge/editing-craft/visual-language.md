# Visual Language — Common Craft

## Shot Size & Angle

- **Wide / Establishing** — context, scale, environment
- **Medium** — subject in context, readable action
- **Close-up** — detail, emotion, evidence
- **Angle** — power, intimacy, objectivity (high, low, eye-level)

## Composition

- **Foreground / middle / background** — depth layers
- **Visual hierarchy** — what reads first
- **Eye trace** — path attention follows across cuts
- **Look direction** — where subjects look (screen direction)
- **Negative space** — breathing room; emphasis by absence
- **Balance** — visual weight distribution

## Continuity (Craft Defaults)

- **180-degree rule** — spatial relationship consistency
- **Match on action** — cut on movement for smoothness
- **Screen direction** — consistent left/right movement when possible
- **Spatial continuity** — audience knows where things are
- **Motion continuity** — action speed feels believable

**Priority note:** Technical continuity is **not** always highest priority.

Visual language ultimately serves:

```
Emotion → Story → Rhythm → Attention
```

## Vertical 9:16

- **Subject-safe reframing** — preserve faces, hands, key objects
- **Fill frame** without unintended letterbox when Brief requires vertical delivery
- **Attention target** higher in frame when subtitles occupy lower third

## Subject Reframing

When reframing landscape to portrait:

- Preserve aspect ratio
- Reposition for subject / action
- Do not stretch
- Do not default to black bars unless intentional design
